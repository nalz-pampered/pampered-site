Pampered

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Pampered is a mobile app that helps people in Kuwait find salons and book appointments. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and what you can do about it.

We do not sell your personal information, and there are no payments inside the app.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details. Your first name and email address when you create an account, plus a password that is stored in encrypted form and is never visible to us.
  • Booking details. The salon, service, staff member, date and time you choose when you make an appointment.
  • Home service details. If you book a service at your home, we collect the phone number, address and any notes you enter so the salon can reach you and find you.
  • Reviews and ratings. Any rating or written review you submit, which is shown publicly in the app next to your first name.
  • Saved salons. The salons you add to your favourites.
  • Messages you send us. Anything you include if you contact us through the app.

Information collected automatically

  • Approximate location. Only if you allow it. We use your device's location to show how far salons are from you and to sort the Nearby list. You can refuse, and the rest of the app still works. We do not store a history of where you have been.
  • Basic technical information needed to run the service, such as the type of device and app version, and records of errors.

How we use your information

  • To create and maintain your account and sign you in.
  • To send your booking to the salon and reserve the time in their calendar.
  • To show you your saved salons and your reviews.
  • To show distances and nearby salons, if you have allowed location access.
  • To respond to you when you contact us.
  • To keep the app working, fix problems, and prevent abuse.

Who we share it with

We share only what is necessary, and only with the following:

  • The salon you book with. When you make an appointment, the salon receives your name, the service and time you chose, and for home service bookings your phone number and address. Salons are independent businesses and handle your details under their own practices.
  • Supabase, which hosts our database and handles sign-in on our behalf.
  • Google Calendar, which stores the appointment in the salon's calendar so their schedule stays accurate.
  • Authorities, if we are legally required to do so.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Payments

There is nothing to buy inside Pampered. The app is free to download and free to use. Salons pay us a monthly membership to be listed, and that is arranged directly with them outside the app. We do not collect or store card or bank details from customers.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information for as long as your account exists. Booking records are kept so that you and the salon have a record of appointments. Reviews stay visible until you delete them or your account.

Your choices

  • Delete your account. You can delete your account and the personal information attached to it at any time from the Account screen in the app, or by emailing us.
  • Access or correct your information. Email us and we will provide a copy or fix anything that is wrong.
  • Location. You can turn location access on or off at any time in your device settings.
  • Reviews. You can remove a review you have written.

Security

Your information is stored on secured servers, passwords are encrypted, and access is limited to what is needed to run the service. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security, but we take it seriously and will tell you if something goes wrong that affects you.

Children

Pampered is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us their information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page, and for significant changes we will let you know in the app.
